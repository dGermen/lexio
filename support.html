Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Lexio works fully as a guest and keeps your progress on your device. Signing in with Google, Apple or email backs it up and syncs it between devices.

The words aren't read aloud. Lexio uses your device's own voices. Check that your phone isn't muted and that a voice for German, French or Italian is installed (iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices; Android: Settings → Text-to-speech). The speaker buttons always play; the "Read aloud automatically" setting only controls automatic playback.

How do I change language or my daily goal? Tap the language name at the top of Home to switch course. Change the daily goal under Account → Settings.

How do I reset or delete my data? Reset a course's progress under Account → Settings → Reset progress. Delete your account and its synced progress under Account → Delete account (shown when signed in), or see how to delete your account.
