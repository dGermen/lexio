Delete your Lexio account

Lexio: Learn Languages · by Yunus Germen · applies to the iOS, Android and web apps

You can delete your Lexio account and all data stored with it at any time. Lexio also works without an account: if you never signed in, everything lives on your device and deleting the app deletes it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to denizgermenaa@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Lexio account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in (or, for Google / Apple sign-in, tell us which one you used). This is how we confirm the request is yours.
  3. You will receive a confirmation reply. The account and its data are deleted within 30 days of that confirmation.

An in-app “Delete account” option is on the way; until then, email is the way to request it.

What is deleted

  • Your account (the Firebase user record: identifier, email address or the identifier Google/Apple shared with us, display name).
  • Your cloud-synced learning progress: words seen, answers, review schedule, streak and per-day activity.
  • Any creator code linked to the account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing identifying you is kept after deletion. Server backups that may still contain the record are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • Progress stored locally on your phone is not affected by a server-side deletion; delete the app or clear its data to remove it.

Only delete some data?

If you want to keep your account but reset your learning progress, use Settings → Reset progress in the app, or email us with what you want removed.

See also the Lexio Privacy Policy.
