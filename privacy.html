Lexio Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026 · Applies to the Lexio apps for iOS and Android and the web app at dgermen.github.io/lexio

Lexio is a language-learning app made by Yunus Germen (“we”). This policy explains what Lexio stores, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: Lexio works without an account and keeps your learning progress on your device; if you sign in, your progress is backed up to our servers so it follows you across devices. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

What Lexio stores

DataWhenWhy
Learning progress (which words you have seen, your answers, review schedule, daily counts, settings such as language and daily goal)Always, on your deviceTo run the spaced-repetition schedule and show your progress
Account identifier and, if you choose email sign-in, your email address and a password hash; if you choose Google or Apple sign-in, the identifier and name/email those providers share with usOnly when you sign inTo recognise you and back up your progress across devices
A copy of your learning progressOnly when you sign inCloud backup and sync
Per-day activity counts (cards answered, days studied)Only when you sign inYour streak and history
A creator code, if you enter oneOnly if you enter oneTo credit the person who recommended Lexio to you; it unlocks nothing

Microphone and speech

If you use the microphone button to speak an answer, Lexio asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. Your speech is transcribed by the speech-recognition service built into your device (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). Lexio only receives the recognised text, uses it to fill in the answer, and does not store audio.

Where data is processed

Sign-in and cloud backup use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase processes this data on Google's behalf under its own terms and security measures. Servers may be located outside your country. Text-to-speech playback happens on your device.

What Lexio does not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or files.

Your choices

  • Use Lexio without an account. Everything stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Sign out at any time from the Account tab; local progress stays on the device.
  • Delete your account and cloud data. Follow the steps at dgermen.github.io/lexio/delete-account.html: email us from the address you signed in with. We remove the account and its stored progress within 30 days.
  • Revoke microphone access in your device settings; typing keeps working.

Children

Lexio is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the new effective date at the top.
